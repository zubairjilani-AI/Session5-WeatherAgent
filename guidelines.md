# Weather safety guidelines
 
## Heat
When daily highs stay above 40 C for two or more days, treat it as a heatwave. Advise people to stay indoors between noon and 4 PM, drink water often, and check on elderly neighbours. Above 45 C is an emergency level.
 
## Wind
Peak winds above 60 km/h are dangerous. Advise people to secure loose objects, avoid driving high-sided vehicles, and stay away from trees and billboards.
 
## Cold
When lows fall below 5 C, advise warm clothing and care for people without heating. Below 0 C, warn about frost on roads.
 
## Rain
Heavy rain over 50 mm in a day can flood low areas. Advise avoiding underpasses and keeping phones charged.
 
## Alert rules
Send an alert only for heat, wind, cold or rain conditions that cross the thresholds in these guidelines. Do not repeat an alert about the same condition on consecutive days.
